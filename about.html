About Wild Cats

Wild Cats is an educational website dedicated to exploring the beauty, diversity, and ecological importance of wild feline species around the world. From lions and tigers to leopards and snow leopards, wild cats play a critical role in maintaining healthy ecosystems. This project aims to spread awareness about their habitats, behaviors, and the threats many species face today due to habitat destruction, climate change, and poaching. Through documentaries, facts, and educational content, this website encourages conservation and appreciation for some of nature’s most extraordinary predators.
